\begin{abstract}
随着现代物流与智能仓储技术的发展，立体仓库库存盘点对自动化、智能化和实时性提出了更高要求。针对传统人工盘点效率低、成本高，且在复杂堆叠场景下易出现漏盘与错盘的问题，本文面向立体仓库堆叠货物盘点任务，围绕复杂场景识别、数量推理与部署适配开展研究，主要包括改进实例分割模型、既有后处理数量估计流程的任务适配以及模型压缩与部署可行性分析三个方面。

针对货物遮挡明显、结构关系复杂以及现有实例分割模型易出现漏检与误检的问题，本文以 YOLO11s-Seg 为基线，提出改进模型 YOLO11-StackLite。该模型从注意力建模、下采样效率和空间结构表达三个方面进行优化，设计了通道拆分注意力模块 C2SASA、重排分组卷积模块 SSConv 和具有四向卷积结构的 C3k2\_QDConv 模块，以增强复杂堆叠场景下的检测与分割能力。

针对仅依赖检测结果难以准确完成库存数量推断的问题，本文承接前期研究中的面积比后处理计数思路，完成其与改进实例分割模型输出的适配与集成。该部分工作的重点在于验证既有计数流程在本文模型输出条件下的适用性，而非重新提出新的计数理论。相关流程通过顶层满载检测和基于掩膜关键点的层间面积比估计，实现对堆叠货物层次结构的判别与数量推理；当仅检测到下层目标、视觉证据不足以支撑可靠推理时，则返回不可判定状态并交由系统复核。

考虑到仓储现场通常具有边缘部署需求，而高精度实例分割模型存在参数量较大、计算复杂度较高的问题，本文进一步开展了面向边缘部署需求的模型压缩研究。针对 YOLO11-StackLite，构建了由稀疏约束训练、结构化物理剪枝、保留通道权重迁移、剪枝后微调恢复以及蒸馏增强组成的轻量化方案，并从模型复杂度与任务指标两方面对其部署可行性进行分析。

在基于文献\cite{hu2025area}中的 Tobacco Cargo Stacking Dataset（TCSD）并经本文整理后得到的 794 张有效图像上开展的实验结果表明：YOLO11-StackLite 在全量验证集上的平均 DA 和平均 CA 分别达到 95.88\% 和 94.87\%，在仅保留顶层未满载有效计数图像的困难场景中，平均 CA 和平均 DA 分别达到 80.90\% 和 83.49\%，整体结果优于主要对比模型；在压缩实验中，物理剪枝模型参数量由 9.4 M 降至 7.8 M，GFLOPs 由 34.9 降至 29.1，经微调恢复后平均 DA 和平均 CA 分别达到 94.82\% 和 93.88\%，经蒸馏增强后进一步达到 95.10\% 和 94.02\%。相关结果表明，本文方法在当前数据集与实验设置下能够较好支持立体仓库堆叠货物盘点任务，并在模型复杂度与任务性能之间取得了较好的平衡，可为后续边缘设备上的实机部署验证提供依据。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
With the rapid development of modern logistics and intelligent warehousing, higher requirements are being placed on the automation, intelligence, and real-time performance of inventory counting in automated warehouses. Traditional manual inventory counting suffers from low efficiency, high labor cost, and poor reliability in complex stacked scenes. To address these problems, this thesis focuses on the inventory counting task of stacked goods in automated warehouses and studies methods for complex-scene recognition, quantity reasoning, and deployment-oriented adaptation. The main work includes an improved instance segmentation model, integration of a post-processing quantity estimation workflow, and model compression with deployment-feasibility analysis.

To address severe occlusion, complex structural relationships, and frequent missed or false detections in stacked cargo scenes, an improved instance segmentation model named YOLO11-StackLite is proposed based on YOLO11s-Seg. The proposed model is optimized from three aspects, namely attention modeling, downsampling efficiency, and spatial structure representation. Specifically, a channel-splitting attention module C2SASA, a shuffle-split convolution module SSConv, and a quad-direction convolution based module C3k2\_QDConv are designed to enhance the detection and segmentation performance in complex stacked scenes.

Since it is difficult to accurately infer the total quantity of goods only from detection results, a prior area-ratio-based post-processing counting scheme is integrated with the proposed instance segmentation model. This workflow includes top-layer full-load detection and an interlayer area-ratio estimation algorithm driven by mask keypoints, which enable structural reasoning and quantity estimation for stacked goods and improve both the interpretability and accuracy of inventory counting.

Considering the demand for edge deployment in warehouse environments and the relatively high parameter scale and computational complexity of high-accuracy instance segmentation models, model compression is further studied for YOLO11-StackLite, and its deployment feasibility is analyzed from the perspective of model complexity and task performance. A lightweight pipeline composed of sparse-constrained training, structured physical pruning, preserved-channel weight transfer, post-pruning fine-tuning, and distillation enhancement is constructed. Specifically, BN scaling factors are regularized to enlarge channel-importance differences, structured physical pruning is applied to key modules such as C2SASA, C3k2\_QDConv, and SSConv under module-aware constraints, and the retained weights are transferred to the compact model. Soft-target distillation and ReviewKD-inspired multi-layer feature distillation are then introduced to compensate for the performance degradation caused by compression, thereby improving the deployment adaptability of the model.

Experiments conducted on 794 valid images organized from the Tobacco Cargo Stacking Dataset (TCSD) in \cite{hu2025area} show that YOLO11-StackLite achieves an average DA of 95.88\% and an average CA of 94.87\% on the full validation set. On the more challenging subset containing only effective counting images with a non-full top layer, the average CA and DA reach 80.90\% and 83.49\%, respectively, and the overall results remain better than those of the main comparison models. In the compression experiment, the physically pruned model reduces the parameter count from 9.4 M to 7.8 M and GFLOPs from 34.9 to 29.1; after fine-tuning, the compact model reaches an average DA of 94.82\% and an average CA of 93.88\%, and after further distillation enhancement, these values improve to 95.10\% and 94.02\%, respectively. These results indicate that the proposed method is effective under the current dataset and experimental setting, achieves a reasonable balance between complexity and task performance, and can provide evidence for subsequent real-device deployment evaluation.
\end{abstract*}
